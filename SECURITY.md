## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you identify a compliance ambiguity, data handling vulnerability, or security flaw:

1. Email directly to: **`sf.foodzeit@googlemail.com`**
2. Include regulatory context, affected sections, and proposed remediation.
3. Response SLA: Initial acknowledgement within 24–48 hours.
